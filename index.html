Administrative Service

4-Step Student Claim & Verification Protocol

To prevent false claims and protect students' privacy, our college follows strict verification guidelines:

1

Locate Item

Browse the portal or use the search bar to locate your missing belonging.

2

File Ownership Claim

Click "Claim Item" and describe unique identifying marks, lockscreen passwords, or serial stamps.

3

Faculty Review

The posting teacher checks your proof against confidential custody records.

4

Pickup & Handover

Receive approval, show your College ID Card at the teacher's cabin, and collect your item!
